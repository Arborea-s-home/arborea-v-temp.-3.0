Reference contexts
Each group includes the contexts with at least one selected taxon after filtering. Geographical filters use the linked site location where recorded. Association records come from those same contexts and pass the same filters. The selected taxa are excluded from their own group's association results.
Presence / absence
The denominator is all reference contexts in the group. Each associated taxon or category contributes one presence per context, even when several occurrences are recorded there.
Quantitative
Bars sum the exact counts of associated remains across the reference contexts. One record contributes its recorded count once. Bounds, ranges, qualitative quantities and weights are excluded. Contexts with mixed reporting contribute the exact counts available.
Qualitative reporting
The table lists contexts with an associated taxon missing an exact count. It displays the recorded entity field. “Absent” indicates no occurrence in the filtered context and “Not declared” indicates an occurrence with an empty entity field.
